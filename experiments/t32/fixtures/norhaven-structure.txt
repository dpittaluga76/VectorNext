# Norhaven Link accepted structure
Synthetic engineering fixture; not a description of a real bridge.

## August 2031 acceptance
The accepted Norhaven Link route has 12 piers. The preliminary west alignment had 10 piers and was not constructed.
A pier is a foundation support. The inspection checklist includes 24 bearing seats; bearing seats are not piers. The traffic authorization is issued separately by the operations office.
